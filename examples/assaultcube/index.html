AssaultCube.
In your browser.

An experimental browser adaptation of AssaultCube 1.3.0.2 by Rabid Viper Productions and contributors. The real engine, maps and offline bots run on your device.

1. Choose your game folder

Use an untouched v1.3.0.2 checkout (download and extract the source ZIP, or follow the build instructions). Select its root folder, which contains packages, config, and bot. Files are verified and read locally; they are never uploaded. Stock media are not included on this site.
